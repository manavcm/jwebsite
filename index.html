FAQs

Questions owners ask us.

Can you manage my property if I live abroad?

Yes. Most of our clients are NRIs and overseas Indians. Everything runs remotely — video inspections, online reports, digital agreements, and rent transferred directly to your account.

Do you collect rent?

Yes. We collect rent every month, follow up on delays, manage deposits, and send you a monthly rent statement.

How are tenants verified?

Every tenant goes through identity, employment and background verification before we prepare a rent agreement.

How often will I receive updates?

Based on your plan — from weekly to annual inspections — plus instant updates whenever anything needs your attention.

Can I approve repair work before it starts?

Always. You receive a quotation with photos first. Work begins only after your approval, with before-and-after proof when done.

How are repair costs managed?

Every expense is pre-approved by you, invoiced by verified contractors, and recorded in your monthly expense statement.

Do you maintain financial records?

Yes — rent received, bills paid, repairs and renewals are all recorded, with monthly statements and an annual financial summary.

Is my property insured?

We assist with insurance selection and handle renewals on your behalf so cover never lapses.

Can I terminate the service anytime?

Yes. Our agreements are transparent with simple exit terms — and no hidden charges.
